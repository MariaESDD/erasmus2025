What We Stand For

Art as a Bridge to Belonging

Inclusion through arts is an approach that uses creative practices to promote social integration, support people with disabilities, migrants, at-risk youth, and anyone facing social exclusion. Art offers a non-verbal language of empathy — a space where difference becomes a strength.

Across Portugal, Germany, Spain, and Turkey, organisations, schools, cultural centres, and NGOs are pioneering programmes that transform lives through painting, theatre, music, dance, and digital arts. These initiatives share a belief: every human being has an inherent capacity for creative expression, and that capacity is a pathway to dignity and inclusion.

Our Initiative

The Project

The project "Inclusion Through Arts" aims to use the arts — Theatre, Music, and Dance — as an educational strategy. The main objective is to carry out pedagogical activities using creative art, dramatic plays and games, improvisation, and traditional choreographies and songs from each country, working on common themes and promoting interculturality and inclusion.

Schools can contribute as an input for pedagogically structured solutions, depending on the educational context, through innovation and creativity, in dialogue and interaction with the community and different partners. Contact with other realities will certainly enrich knowledge through experience, allowing the reinvention of proposals and actions by students in their own reality.

It is intended that the involved partners share their experiences and traditions through artistic expression, promoting characteristic presentations of each country, and also conducting collaborative and multicultural dynamics in small international groups. Through a variety of artistic activities, students can improve their personal skills — creativity, autonomy, self-confidence, self-awareness, and acceptance of oneself and others.

Across Europe & Beyond

What Is Happening

Four countries, four stories. Each exploring how arts create social inclusion in its own unique cultural context.

🇵🇹 Portugal

Portugal, located on the western edge of the Iberian Peninsula, is a seafaring nation famous for its sun-drenched coastlines, warm hospitality, and historic charm. With a rich legacy rooted in the Age of Discovery, the country offers a unique blend of ancient tradition and contemporary Mediterranean lifestyle. Beyond its iconic golden beaches in the Algarve and rolling vineyards along the Douro River, Portugal has earned international praise for its safety, affordable cost of living, and thriving tech scene, establishing itself as a favorite destination for Erasmus students.

Perched on seven hills overlooking the Tejo River, Lisbon is Europe’s second-oldest capital city and a vibrant epicenter of maritime history. The city beautifully bridges old-world charm, seen in the narrow, cobblestone alleyways of the Alfama district, with forward-thinking modernity and an energetic student atmosphere. Visitors are drawn to its stunning viewpoints, historic yellow trams, and lively cultural venues where traditional Fado music fills the night air alongside a bustling creative economy.

🇵🇹Portugal

🇩🇪 Germany

Germany, situated in the heart of Europe, is a major political and economic powerhouse renowned for its rich history, technological innovation, and diverse landscapes. From the financial hub of Frankfurt to the scenic Bavarian Alps and the dense Black Forest, the country seamlessly blends modern efficiency with deep cultural traditions. Germany is also celebrated worldwide for its academic excellence, sustainable initiatives, and contributions to philosophy, classical music, and science, making it a highly attractive destination for international students and researchers alike.

At the center of this dynamic nation lies Berlin, the capital city that stands as a profound symbol of resilience and transformation. Once divided by the Berlin Wall, the modern metropolis has evolved into a global hub for creativity, nightlife, and cultural diversity. Students visiting Berlin can explore landmark historical sites like the Brandenburg Gate and Checkpoint Charlie, while simultaneously immersing themselves in a thriving contemporary arts scene, startups, and open-minded community spaces.

🇩🇪Germany

🇪🇸 Spain

Spain is a diverse and captivating country occupying most of the Iberian Peninsula, celebrated for its passionate culture, world-class gastronomy, and vibrant architectural heritage. Shaped by a unique mix of Roman, Moorish, and Christian historical influences, the nation varies dramatically from the green cliffs of Galicia to the lively beaches of Andalusia. Spain’s relaxed lifestyle, emphasis on outdoor socializing, and world-renowned festivals make it one of the most culturally immersive environments in Europe, offering an unforgettable experience for international youth.

In the exact geographic center of the country lies Madrid, a majestic capital city famous for its artistic wealth, grand avenues, and tireless energy. Home to the legendary "Golden Triangle of Art" museums—including the Prado—Madrid seamlessly matches its royal history with a fast-paced modern economy. For students, the city is a paradise of social life, offering vast green parks like El Retiro, traditional tapas markets, and local neighborhoods that truly come alive after dark.

🇪🇸Spain

🇹🇷 Turkey

Turkey (officially Türkiye) is a fascinating transcontinental country that bridges Europe and Asia, serving as a historic crossroads of civilizations for millennia. Home to remnants of the Byzantine and Ottoman Empires, the country boasts an incredibly rich cultural tapestry, world-famous hospitality, and diverse landscapes ranging from Mediterranean beaches to the fairy-chimney rock formations of Cappadocia. This unique fusion of Eastern and Western traditions creates an exceptionally vibrant, welcoming, and educational environment for students looking to expand their global perspective.

While Istanbul is the largest economic hub, Ankara is the modern capital and political heart of the nation, strategically positioned in central Anatolia. Chosen as the capital in 1923 by the republic's founder, Mustafa Kemal Atatürk, Ankara is a well-planned city characterized by major universities, government institutions, and diplomatic headquarters. International visitors can explore deep layers of history at the ancient Ankara Citadel and the Museum of Anatolian Civilizations, or pay respects at Anıtkabir, the monumental mausoleum of Atatürk, making it an ideal center for academic and cultural discovery.

Partner Cities

Discover Our Cities

Four cities across Europe, each with a unique culture and history — united through arts and inclusion.

🇵🇹 Coimbra, Portugal

Coimbra features a deeply authentic immersion into one of Europe’s most historic academic environments. Known worldwide as the "City of Knowledge," it is home to one of the oldest universities in continuous operation. Visitors can wander through its steep, medieval streets and experience the unique academic traditions, including the traditional university uniforms that have shaped the local identity for centuries.

🇩🇪 Aschaffenburg, Germany

Nestled along the scenic banks of the River Main, Aschaffenburg offered a perfect balance between architectural splendor and modern innovation. Often referred to as the "Bavarian Nice" due to its mild climate and Mediterranean lifestyle flair, the city charmed our participants with the grand red-sandstone Schloss Johannisburg and its beautiful green parks. The city offers a perfect balance of traditional Bavarian architecture and efficient modern infrastructure.

🇪🇸 Granada, Spain

Granada provided a captivating backdrop for our project, where the historical coexistence of Christian and Moorish cultures is visible in every corner. Framed by the stunning Sierra Nevada mountains, students were mesmerized by the breathtaking views of the Alhambra and the narrow, white-washed streets of the Albaicín district. It is also highly regarded for its local culinary culture and tapas.

🇹🇷 Kahramanmaraş, Turkey

Located at the foot of the Taurus Mountains, Kahramanmaraş is a historic destination rich in culture and tradition. The city is well-known for its ancient castle, traditional craftsmanship, and local markets. It is also famous across the country for its unique, chewy local ice cream, called Maraş Dondurma.
